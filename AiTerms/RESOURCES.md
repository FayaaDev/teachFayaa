# AI Badges Associate Exam Resources

## Knowledge

- [Google: Machine Learning Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course)
  Authoritative interactive overview. Use for: classification, regression, overfitting, metrics, data preparation, neural networks, and production ML.
- [scikit-learn: Model evaluation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html)
  Precise metric definitions and evaluation guidance. Use for: precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, cross-validation, and selecting the right score.
- [NIST: AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)
  High-trust framework for trustworthy and responsible AI. Use for: governance, fairness, privacy, explainability, and human oversight.
- [SDAIA: Saudi Academic Framework for AI Qualifications](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/Research/Documents/SaudiAcademicFramework.pdf)
  Saudi AI curriculum framework. Use for: the foundational areas expected at an associate level: computing, programming, statistics, ML, optimization, deep learning, NLP, vision, and ethics.
