# Mission: Pass the AI Badges associate-level AI/ML exam

## Why
Pass the AI Badges associate-level assessment today and be able to choose, explain, and evaluate practical AI/ML approaches rather than relying on generated code alone.

## Success looks like
- Correctly classify common AI/ML scenarios and justify the choice in exam-style questions.
- Recall the core ML, LLM, MLOps, and responsible-AI terms in `terms.txt` under time pressure.
- Identify misleading metric, deployment, and governance choices in scenario questions.

## Constraints
- Exam is in approximately three hours.
- New to machine learning; experienced with "vibe coding."
- Prioritize recognition, retrieval, and practical scenario reasoning over implementation detail.

## Out of scope
- Training production models from scratch.
- Mathematical derivations beyond the intuition needed to answer associate-level questions.
