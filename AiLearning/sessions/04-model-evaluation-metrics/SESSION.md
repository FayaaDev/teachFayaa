# Session 04: Model Evaluation — Metrics

**Status:** Locked until prior-session evidence  

## Outcome

Choose a metric from asymmetric error costs, compute classification metrics, set thresholds from operational constraints, and know when precision-recall is more informative than ROC.

## Study map

- Confusion matrix
- Accuracy, precision, recall, and F-scores
- ROC versus precision-recall
- Decision thresholds
- Regression metrics

## Study materials

- [Metrics and Scoring — scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html) — Covers confusion matrices, accuracy, precision, recall, F-scores, ROC AUC, and regression metrics.
- [Classification: Accuracy, Recall, Precision, and Related Metrics — Google](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/classification/accuracy-precision-recall) — Covers interpreting classification metrics under asymmetric error costs.
- [Classification Thresholds — Google](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/classification/thresholding) — Covers choosing decision thresholds from operational constraints.
- [ROC and AUC — Google](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/classification/roc-and-auc) — Covers ROC curves and their limitations for imbalanced classification.
- [Precision-Recall Curves — Google](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/classification/precision-recall) — Covers when precision-recall is more informative than ROC.
