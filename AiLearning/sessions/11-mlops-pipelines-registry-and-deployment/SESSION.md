# Session 11: MLOps — Pipelines, Registry & Deployment

**Status:** Locked until prior-session evidence  

## Outcome

Reproduce experiments, choose serving and release strategies, enforce quality gates, and prevent training-serving skew structurally.

## Study map

- Reproducibility
- Experiment tracking and registry
- Feature stores and skew
- Continuous integration, delivery, and training
- Serving and release patterns

## Study materials

- [Google Cloud: MLOps, continuous delivery and automation pipelines](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/mlops-continuous-delivery-and-automation-pipelines-in-machine-learning) — Covers reproducibility, pipelines, CI/CD/CT, feature stores, skew, and serving delivery.
- [Sculley et al.: Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/86df7dcfd896fcaf2674f757a2463eba-Abstract.html) — Covers MLOps failure modes and why pipelines require structural controls.
- [MLflow Tracking](https://mlflow.org/docs/latest/ml/tracking/) — Covers experiment tracking, reproducibility metadata, and run comparison.
- [MLflow Model Registry](https://mlflow.org/docs/latest/ml/model-registry/) — Covers versioned model registration, promotion, rollback, and release governance.
