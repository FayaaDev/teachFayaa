# Session 06: Deep Learning Foundations

**Status:** Locked until prior-session evidence  

## Outcome

Explain non-linearity, diagnose training curves, select optimizers and regularization, choose convolutional or recurrent architectures from data structure, and diagnose vanishing gradients.

## Study map

- Neurons and activation functions
- Backpropagation and optimizers
- Neural-network regularization
- Convolutional networks
- Sequences and vanishing gradients

## Study materials

- [Deep Feedforward Networks — Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/mlp.html) — Covers neurons, activation functions, and backpropagation.
- [Regularization for Deep Learning — Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/regularization.html) — Covers neural-network regularization and interpreting training curves.
- [Optimization for Training Deep Models — Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/optimization.html) — Covers optimizers, gradient descent, and vanishing or exploding gradients.
- [Convolutional Networks — Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/convnets.html) — Covers convolutional architectures for spatial data.
- [Sequence Modeling: Recurrent and Recursive Nets — Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/rnn.html) — Covers recurrent architectures, sequences, and long-term gradient problems.
