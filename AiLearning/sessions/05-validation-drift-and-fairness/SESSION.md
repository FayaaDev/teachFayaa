# Session 05: Validation, Drift & Fairness

**Status:** Locked until prior-session evidence  

## Outcome

Choose validation that respects temporal, grouped, or imbalanced structure; distinguish data drift from concept drift; measure fairness by group and connect it to SDAIA principles.

## Study map

- Train, validation, and test splits
- Cross-validation variants
- L1 and L2 regularization
- Class imbalance
- Data drift and concept drift
- Fairness

## Study materials

- [Cross-validation: Evaluating Estimator Performance — scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html) — Covers train, validation, and test splits; grouped, stratified, and time-series cross-validation.
- [Linear Models — scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/linear_model.html) — Covers L1 and L2 regularization.
- [Imbalanced-Learn User Guide](https://imbalanced-learn.org/stable/user_guide.html) — Covers class imbalance and resampling strategies.
- [Learning under Concept Drift: A Review — Gama et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04806) — Covers the distinction between data drift and concept drift.
- [Fairness in Machine Learning — Fairlearn](https://fairlearn.org/main/user_guide/fairness_in_machine_learning.html) — Covers group fairness assessment and its unavoidable tradeoffs.
