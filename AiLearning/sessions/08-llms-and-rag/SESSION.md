# Session 08: Generative AI — LLMs & RAG

**Status:** Locked until prior-session evidence  

## Outcome

Explain hallucination, distinguish knowledge problems from behavior problems, design a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline, and diagnose retrieval failures.

## Study map

- What an LLM does
- Prompt engineering
- RAG pipeline
- Chunking, hybrid search, and reranking
- RAG versus fine-tuning

## Study materials

- [NIST: Retrieval-augmented generation](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/retrieval_augmented_generation) — Covers the LLM/RAG distinction and why retrieval changes knowledge without retraining.
- [Lewis et al.: Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401) — Covers the core RAG pipeline and retrieval failure modes.
- [Anthropic: Prompt engineering overview](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview) — Covers prompt engineering and separating instruction design from knowledge updates.
- [TREC 2025 RAG Track overview](https://trec.nist.gov/pubs/trec34/papers/Overview_rag.pdf) — Covers chunking, hybrid retrieval, reranking, grounding, and RAG evaluation.
