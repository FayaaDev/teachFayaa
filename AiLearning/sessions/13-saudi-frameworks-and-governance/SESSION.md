# Session 13: Saudi Frameworks & AI Governance

**Status:** Locked until prior-session evidence  

## Outcome

Recall SDAIA's seven principles, classify AI risk, apply Saudi personal-data requirements, and connect engineering controls to governance obligations.

## Study map

- SDAIA AI ethics principles
- AI risk levels
- Personal Data Protection Law
- Model cards
- Human oversight

## Study materials

- [SDAIA: AI Ethics Principles and Controls](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Documents/ai-principles.pdf) — Covers the seven principles, risk levels, lifecycle controls, and human accountability.
- [Saudi Personal Data Protection Law](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Documents/PersonalDataProtectionLaw.pdf) — Covers lawful processing, data-subject rights, controller duties, and personal-data safeguards.
- [SDAIA: Guide to the Saudi PDPL](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/wcm/connect/f579bc32-fda8-47bd-bc6f-66b8cb77985c/ENG-Guide+to+the+saudi+PDP+law+for+controllersprocessors.pdf?MOD=AJPERES) — Covers practical PDPL compliance, governance roles, and impact assessments.
- [SDAIA: Generative AI Guidelines for Government](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Files/GenAIGuidelinesForGovernmentENCompressed.pdf) — Covers risk classification, human review, and controls for GenAI use.
- [Mitchell et al.: Model Cards for Model Reporting](https://research.google/pubs/model-cards-for-model-reporting/) — Covers model cards as documentation for transparency, evaluation, and oversight.
