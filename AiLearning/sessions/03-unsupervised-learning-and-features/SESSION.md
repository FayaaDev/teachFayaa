# Session 03: Unsupervised Learning & Feature Engineering

**Status:** Ready to study  

## Outcome

Choose clustering from cluster shape, select categorical encodings from cardinality and model type, detect five leakage forms, and prevent leakage structurally with a pipeline.

## Study map

- Clustering and similarity
- Dimensionality reduction
- Encoding and scaling
- Data leakage
- Feature engineering

## Study materials

- [Clustering — scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/clustering.html) — Covers clustering, similarity, and selecting methods from cluster shape.
- [Unsupervised Dimensionality Reduction — scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/unsupervised_reduction.html) — Covers dimensionality reduction and PCA as a preprocessing step.
- [Preprocessing Data — scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/preprocessing.html) — Covers encoding, scaling, and feature transformations.
- [Common Pitfalls and Recommended Practices — scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html) — Covers data leakage prevention through pipelines and train-only fitting.
