## Study materials

- [Attention Is All You Need — Vaswani et al.](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/7181-attention-is-all-you-need.pdf) — Covers transformers versus recurrence, query/key/value, multi-head attention, positional encodings, and encoder-decoder architecture.
- [BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding — Devlin et al.](https://aclanthology.org/N19-1423/) — Covers encoder-only transformer families and transfer learning.
- [Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners — Radford et al.](https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf) — Covers decoder-only transformer families and transfer learning.
- [Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer — Raffel et al.](https://jmlr.org/papers/v21/20-074.html) — Covers encoder-decoder transformer families, transfer learning, and cumulative review across task forms.
