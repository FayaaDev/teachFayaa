# Session 12: Infrastructure, Monitoring & Security

**Status:** Locked until prior-session evidence  

## Outcome

Choose compute and scaling from latency and cost, define SLI/SLO/SLA correctly, monitor four system layers, and match AI-specific threats to defenses.

## Study map

- CPU, GPU, and TPU
- Scaling and cold starts
- Reliability targets and degradation
- Four monitoring layers
- Access control and AI threats

## Study materials

- [TensorFlow: Use a GPU](https://www.tensorflow.org/guide/gpu) — Covers accelerator selection and the practical CPU/GPU trade-off.
- [Google Cloud: TPU documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/tpu/docs/intro-to-tpu) — Covers TPU architecture and workloads suited to it.
- [Kubernetes: Horizontal Pod Autoscaling](https://kubernetes.io/docs/tasks/run-application/horizontal-pod-autoscale/) — Covers scaling behavior and capacity signals.
- [Google SRE: Implementing SLOs](https://sre.google/workbook/implementing-slos/) — Covers SLI, SLO, SLA, error budgets, degradation decisions, and service-layer monitoring.
- [OWASP ML Security Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-machine-learning-security-top-10/) — Covers AI-specific data, model, and supply-chain threats with corresponding controls.
