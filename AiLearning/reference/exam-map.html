AI Engineer Expert: exam map

What the official source confirms, what the preparation site adds, and how the fourteen sessions cover the role.

Officially confirmed

  • The badge covers designing, building, deploying, and operating AI systems in production.
  • The exam is in person at an approved testing center.
  • Questions assess theory, applied skills, problem analysis, and responses to professional scenarios.
  • The official levels are Assistant, Associate, Expert.
  • The awarded level uses both the overall score and a required threshold at each question level.
  • Applicants must demonstrate English proficiency.

Not publicly confirmed

  • Question count, duration, numeric thresholds, and retake policy.
  • That every question is scenario-based.
  • That the prep site's fourteen sessions or its questions are official.
  • The prep site's exact `D1-D7` weights. Preserve them as study priorities, not verified exam percentages.
