# Session 1 reasoning baseline established

The learner can distinguish semi-supervised, self-supervised, and unsupervised learning from the source of the supervisory signal; choose ranking when operational capacity is fixed; and recognize proxy mismatch. Prediction horizons initially transferred as a memorized number rather than being recomputed from intervention time, and the deterministic-rule decision was initially attributed to human review rather than rule certainty, so both distinctions should recur through spaced, interleaved questions.

## Evidence

Correct responses were given for self-supervised token masking, fixed-capacity ranking, and complaint behavior as a mismatched proxy. The learner reached the correct minimum horizon after guided contrast and correctly restated that a deterministic regulation does not require ML.

## Implications

Session 2 can begin. Future quizzes should revisit prediction horizons, deterministic rules, and model-versus-business metrics without repeating the original wording.
