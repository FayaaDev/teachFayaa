# Teaching Notes

- The learner wants one folder per session and plans to study each session before asking questions or taking a quiz.
- Target badge: AI Engineer Expert.
- Use English-first exam terminology. Ask whether Arabic, English, or bilingual explanations are preferred before expanding Session 1.
- Use retrieval practice, spaced review, and mixed professional scenarios. Do not infer mastery from reading completion.
- Never present the prep site's 14 sessions, difficulty labels, or weights as official SDAIA exam specifications.
- Official English badge levels are Assistant, Associate, and Expert. The prep site's use of “Practitioner” for `مشارك` is not the official English label.
- Prefer plain-language explanations, everyday examples, and one short retrieval question at a time before adding technical detail.
- When quizzing after a lesson, use novel scenarios rather than repeating the lesson's embedded questions or transfer prompt.
- Lessons should be more informative and include concrete, worked examples (not just abstract rules) while remaining short enough to fit working memory.
