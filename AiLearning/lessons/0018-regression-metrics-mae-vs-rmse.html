Regression metrics — MAE vs RMSE

Your tangible win: choose MAE or RMSE based on whether large errors hurt more than small ones.

Worked example: delivery-time prediction

A logistics company predicts delivery time in minutes. An error of 5 minutes is acceptable; an error of 30 minutes causes a customer to miss a connecting flight. The company therefore penalises large errors more heavily than small ones.

Memory rule: use RMSE when large errors are disproportionately expensive; use MAE when every minute of error costs the same.
