Spot the leak

Your tangible win: name the exact record that leaks future information in a proposed split.

The single rule

Training data must never contain information that would only be known after the validation period begins. Any record whose timestamp is later than the first validation timestamp is future leakage.

Memory rule: if its date is after the validation start, it cannot be in training.
