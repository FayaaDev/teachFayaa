ROC-AUC — ranking quality

Your tangible win: state what AUC actually measures when you change the decision threshold.

Worked example: loan default model

A bank’s loan model outputs a probability of default for each applicant. The bank can set the approval threshold at 10 %, 20 %, or 30 % default risk. At every possible threshold the model produces a different pair of true-positive and false-positive rates. The AUC tells how well the model ranks risky applicants above safe ones across all possible thresholds — without committing to one cutoff yet.

Memory rule: AUC = probability that a randomly chosen positive example is ranked higher than a randomly chosen negative example.
