Precision vs Recall — the cost decides

Your tangible win: given a concrete cost ratio, name whether you should optimise precision or recall first.

Worked example: cancer screening

A hospital runs a screening test on 10 000 patients. 80 patients actually have early-stage cancer. Missing one cancer case (false negative) costs SAR 120 000 in later treatment and lawsuits. A false positive only costs SAR 800 for a follow-up biopsy. The hospital therefore accepts many extra biopsies to avoid missing even one cancer case.

Memory rule: when the cost of a false negative is much higher than a false positive, optimise recall first.
