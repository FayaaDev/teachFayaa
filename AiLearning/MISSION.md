# Mission: Earn the AI Engineer Expert Badge

## Why
Prepare to earn SDAIA's AI Engineer Expert badge and demonstrate senior-level judgement in designing, building, deploying, securing, and operating AI systems in production.

## Success looks like
- Explain and apply all topics in the 14-session preparation curriculum without relying on recognition alone.
- Solve unfamiliar professional scenarios by identifying the governing constraint before choosing an answer.
- Demonstrate consistently strong retrieval and reasoning on Expert-level questions across every covered domain.
- Identify weak areas from quizzes and repair them through spaced review before the exam.

## Constraints
- Follow the 14-session sequence from `sdaia.qarni.me`, one folder per session.
- Each study cycle is: study, ask questions, complete a closed-book quiz, then review errors.
- Use English-first technical terminology because English proficiency is an official registration requirement; explanations can switch to Arabic when requested.
- The exam date and next registration window are not yet known. The official badge record checked on 2026-09-05 marks the 2026-07-16 to 2026-08-10 application window as ended.
- SDAIA has not publicly disclosed the question count, duration, numeric level thresholds, or retake policy in the sources checked.

## Out of scope
- Preparing for the Data Scientist badge.
- Treating the third-party preparation site, its questions, or its domain weights as an official exam blueprint.
